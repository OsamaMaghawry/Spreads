About

Software for options traders who trade in their own brokerage account

DeltaMint helps you find trades that match rules you set, send and work those orders with your broker, and read every position as the structure you traded — through to the closed record. Your money and your positions stay at your broker the whole time.

What it does

Strategy ScannerFind

You set the rules — strategy, days to expiry, short-strike delta, wing width, minimum credit, maximum risk — and the Scanner sweeps a list of tickers, expiry by expiry, for setups that meet them. It builds put and call credit spreads, iron condors, cash-secured puts and covered calls, including covered calls written against a long call you already hold.

  • Priced at the side that trades. A credit is the short bid less the long ask, not the midpoint.
  • Earnings flagged. A report due before expiry is shown on the row, not hidden by a filter.
  • Nothing skipped silently. A ticker that produced no setup says which rule excluded it.

Results are ranked by return on risk so you can sort what matched your filters. The ranking is arithmetic on your rules, not a recommendation.

Option chainFind

Every listed strike and expiry for a ticker, with the current price marked between the in-the-money and out-of-the-money strikes. Pick one contract for a single option, or two for a vertical, calendar or diagonal spread, and open the same ticket the Scanner uses.

The order ticketTrade

Before anything is sent you see every leg quoted live, the most the position can lose, its break-even, a payoff chart, and the order as a share of your account.

  • You choose the price. Set a limit and it rests where you put it, or let the ticket work toward a fill in small steps — never past the floor you set.
  • Checked again at the moment you send. If the stock has moved, a short strike is now in the money, or the shares behind a covered call already cover another call, the ticket says so and waits for you.
  • Nothing is sent without your confirmation. Every order goes to your broker and is executed there, under your broker's terms.
DashboardTrack

A broker lists your account one leg at a time. The Dashboard groups those legs back into what you traded — spreads, iron condors, cash-secured puts, covered calls and shares from assignment — and shows each one on a strike ladder with its break-even, maximum risk, capital tied up and live price.

  • Cost basis that remembers. Shares from an assigned put show the basis less the premiums collected on that name, labelled as adjusted beside the broker's own figure.
  • Close any way you need. Close a whole structure or a single leg, at a price you set or worked toward a fill.
  • Honest when it cannot know. A figure without a trusted price shows a dash, and an uncovered call is shown as having no ceiling on its loss.
Trade History and AnalysisReview

Closed trades are rebuilt from your broker's own account activity, including assignment, exercise and expiry, and each result is split into premium, early close and assignment. Analysis turns that record into statistics — win rate, return on risk, drawdown, credit kept — by month, by ticker and by strategy, with a PDF export.

These figures are for your own review. They are not tax reporting; your broker's 1099 is the record for that.

How it connects to your broker

You link your brokerage account through your broker's own sign-in and consent screen. DeltaMint never sees your brokerage password, and the access it is given is encrypted at rest. Paper and live accounts both connect, and you can disconnect at any time, from DeltaMint or from your broker.

Your money and securities stay with your broker. DeltaMint reads your positions and sends the orders you confirm; your broker holds the account and executes every trade.

What DeltaMint is not

  • Not a broker-dealer. DeltaMint does not hold funds or securities, open or maintain brokerage accounts, or execute trades itself.
  • Not investment advice. Nothing in DeltaMint recommends a security, a strategy or a trade. What appears is what matched rules you set.
  • No signals or copy trading. There is no following other traders and no one else's trades placed in your account.
  • No action you did not set. Where DeltaMint adjusts a working order, it carries out limits you configured, at your direction.

Who it is for

People who already trade options in their own account and want their book to read the way they traded it: sellers of premium through spreads, iron condors and the wheel, and anyone tired of reconstructing a four-legged position from four separate lines on a broker's screen.

Options involve substantial risk and are not suitable for every investor. Before trading, read Characteristics and Risks of Standardized Options.
