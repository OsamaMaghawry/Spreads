Free options calculators

Eight calculators for the numbers options traders otherwise work out by hand: max loss, breakevens, returns, the expected move and assignment risk. Each one shows the formula it uses and a worked example. Nothing to sign up for.

  • Credit spread calculatorMax profit, max loss, breakeven and return on risk for a put or call credit spread.Open calculator
  • Iron condor calculatorBoth breakevens, the max loss on either wing and the profit zone.Open calculator
  • Covered call calculatorReturn if called and if the stock stays flat, both annualized, plus your downside cushion.Open calculator
  • Cash secured put calculatorReturn on the cash held, annualized, and your cost basis if assigned.Open calculator
  • Expected move calculatorThe one standard deviation range from implied volatility or a straddle price.Open calculator
  • Annualized return calculatorTurn a premium and the days it takes into a yearly rate, simple and compounded.Open calculator
  • Option roll calculatorThe net credit or debit of a roll, and where your breakeven moves.Open calculator
  • Early assignment risk calculatorCompare a dividend with a short call's time value before the ex-dividend date.Open calculator

How these calculators work

  • Per share, like a quote. Strikes, premiums and credits are entered per share, the way option prices are quoted. One contract is 100 shares, and every dollar figure is multiplied out for you.
  • At expiration. Max profit, max loss and breakevens are what the position is worth on expiration day. Before then it trades at its market price.
  • Formulas on the page. Each calculator lists the formula behind every number and walks through an example, so you can check the result by hand.
  • No fees, no odds. Commissions are not included, and none of the calculators estimates the chance a trade makes money.

Which calculator do I need?

Selling a spread
The credit spread calculator for one side, the iron condor calculator for both.
Running the wheel
The cash secured put calculator before assignment, the covered call calculator after it.
Comparing trades
The annualized return calculator puts different expirations on one scale.
Picking strikes
The expected move calculator shows how far the market prices the stock to move.
Managing a position
The option roll calculator for a roll, the early assignment calculator before an ex-dividend date.
