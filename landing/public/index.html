> the stack

01_screen

Filter by delta and width

Set delta band, spread width, and DTE once. The scanner lists the put spreads, call spreads, and iron condors on your watchlist that match those filters, showing return on risk for each so you can compare them yourself.

02_execute

Route multi-leg orders

Every leg of the spread routes together to your linked brokerage account. Preview the structure before it fires, with a two-step arm-and-confirm on every order.

03_manage

Close from one dialog

Close full spreads or single legs, walk a limit price toward the mark, check open orders, and pull the order log across every connected account without switching screens.
