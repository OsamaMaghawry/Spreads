Questions

Straight answers.

Do you hold my money or my positions?

No. Everything stays at your broker. DeltaMint signs in with your login, reads your account, and sends only the orders you confirm. It never holds funds or securities.

Does it trade on its own?

No. Nothing reaches the broker until you press Submit and confirm it. The walk reprices an order you placed, inside limits you set, and you can stop it at any time.

Is this investment advice?

No. The screener lists what matches the filters you set and shows the numbers behind it. Nothing is recommended. The decision is yours.

Which brokers work?

The brokerage shown above, live and paper accounts. Sign in with your brokerage login and disconnect any time from the Accounts page.

Where do the prices come from?

Live quotes from your broker's market data. Every setup is priced at the side that actually trades and cross-checked against the stock. If the two disagree, it says so rather than showing a number it can't stand behind.

What if I close the ticket while an order is working?

The order keeps working at the broker. Change its price or cancel it from the Orders tab whenever you like.

What does it cost?

Paper accounts are free with no time limit. Live accounts are a flat monthly subscription. Nothing per trade, nothing on results, and you can always close a position on any plan.

Can I try it without risking anything?

Yes. Connect a paper account and use every screen with paper money for as long as you want.
