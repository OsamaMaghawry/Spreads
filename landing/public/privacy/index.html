Privacy Policy

Last updated September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information DeltaMint collects, how it's used, and how it's protected when you use the DeltaMint application (the "Service").

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: the email address you register with.
  • Brokerage connection: the access token issued by your broker when you authorize DeltaMint to connect to your account, and that account's number. We never see or store your brokerage account password.
  • Trading data: positions, orders, account balances, and trade history retrieved from your connected brokerage account(s) in order to display them back to you and compute analytics (P/L, risk, Scanner results).
  • Support records: notes and a status we may keep internally about your account when handling support or managing our relationship with you.
  • Usage data: basic application logs needed to operate and troubleshoot the Service.

2. How we use it

We use this information solely to operate the Service: authenticating you, displaying your positions and account data, running the trades and orders you direct, maintaining your trade history, and providing you with support. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. How it's stored

Data is stored with our backend infrastructure provider using industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and access controls that restrict each user's data to that user alone. Brokerage tokens and any stored credentials are encrypted, held server-side, and are never exposed to other users or sent to your browser.

4. Third parties

To provide the Service, we share the minimum necessary data with:

  • Your broker — to place orders and retrieve account and market data on your behalf, using the authorization you granted through the broker's own connection flow.
  • Our payment processor — to bill paid subscriptions. Your card details go directly to the processor; we never receive or store your card number.
  • Our infrastructure providers (database and authentication, hosting, source control and backup storage) — to run the Service. These providers do not use your data for their own purposes.
  • Our website analytics providers — Google Analytics and Hotjar — to understand how this public website is used, so we can improve it. These run on our public marketing pages and blog only. They are not present in the application itself, so your positions, orders, balances and trade history are never seen by them.

    What they receive on those public pages is: pages visited, approximate location from your IP address, device and browser, the link that referred you, and a record of on-page activity such as scrolling, mouse movement and clicks. Some of that activity is aggregated into heatmaps showing which parts of a page draw attention; some of it is kept as an individual replay of a single visit, which a member of our team may watch directly to see how a page was used. Those public pages contain no forms or input fields, so there is nothing typed for a replay to capture.

    These providers set their own cookies or similar identifiers on this website in order to group activity into a session. Those identifiers are separate from your DeltaMint sign-in and are not linked to your account.

Apart from the analytics providers named above, we identify these by category rather than by name. The current list of named providers is maintained internally and is available on request to a customer, counterparty, or regulator.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

5. Your choices

You can disconnect a brokerage account at any time from the Accounts page, which removes the stored token. You can request deletion of your DeltaMint account and associated data by contacting us.

If you are visiting from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Gibraltar, Jersey, Guernsey or the Isle of Man, the website analytics described in section 4 do not run at all. Neither Google Analytics nor Hotjar is loaded, no analytics cookie or identifier is set, and no session is recorded. We determine your country from the network request itself, at the edge of our own site — no additional third party is contacted to make that decision. If the country cannot be determined, the analytics do not run.

Everywhere else, those analytics can be blocked by any standard content or tracking blocker, and we do not attempt to detect or work around one. Nothing about the Service depends on them: blocking them changes what we can measure, not what you can do.

6. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the Service. Trade history may be retained for your own recordkeeping purposes even after an account is disconnected, until you request deletion. When data is deleted it is removed from the live Service immediately; copies held in routine backups age out within 30 days.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or requests to delete your data can be sent to support@deltamint.app.
