Privacy Policy

Last updated August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information DeltaMint collects, how it's used, and how it's protected when you use the DeltaMint application (the "Service").

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: the email address you register with.
  • Brokerage connection: either an Alpaca API key/secret pair you provide directly, or an OAuth access token issued by Alpaca when you authorize DeltaMint to connect to your account. We never see or store your Alpaca account password.
  • Trading data: positions, orders, account balances, and trade history retrieved from your connected Alpaca account(s) in order to display them back to you and compute analytics (P/L, risk, screener results).
  • Usage data: basic application logs needed to operate and troubleshoot the Service.

2. How we use it

We use this information solely to operate the Service: authenticating you, displaying your positions and account data, running the trades and orders you direct, and maintaining your trade history. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. How it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase, our backend infrastructure provider, using industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and access controls that restrict each user's data to that user alone. API keys, secrets, and OAuth tokens are stored server-side and are never exposed to other users.

4. Third parties

To provide the Service, we share the minimum necessary data with:

  • Alpaca Securities LLC — to place orders and retrieve account/market data on your behalf, using the credentials or OAuth authorization you provided.
  • Our infrastructure providers (database, hosting) — to run the Service. These providers do not use your data for their own purposes.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

5. Your choices

You can disconnect a brokerage account at any time from the Accounts page, which removes the stored credentials/token. You can request deletion of your DeltaMint account and associated data by contacting us.

6. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the Service. Trade history may be retained for your own recordkeeping purposes even after an account is disconnected, until you request deletion.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or requests to delete your data can be sent to the contact address listed on our website.
