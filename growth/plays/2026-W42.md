## The play

Turn the instruments on and read them: the owner sets the one CI secret
(`GOOGLE_METRICS_SA`) and the GA measurement-ID variable that
`docs/ops/queue.md` has carried as `needs owner` since 2 Sep, so the daily
snapshot starts reporting impressions, clicks and sessions. Then seo-editor
works the 31 live posts from real numbers: pages with impressions but a click
rate under 1 % get a title and meta rewrite; pages with none get an indexing
check, and the first measured visitor-to-signup rate replaces the plan's 3 %
benchmark.
