Pricing schedule

Last updated September 24, 2026

DeltaMint is software sold by subscription. This schedule lists every fee DeltaMint charges. Fees are for access to the software only.

1. Plans and fees

PlanPriceBillingWhat it covers
Paper$0No chargeThe full product against paper (simulated) brokerage accounts. No time limit, no usage limit.
Live — monthly$29 / monthBilled monthly, in advanceThe same product, with orders placed in live brokerage accounts.
Live — yearly$290 / yearBilled yearly, in advanceAs Live — monthly, paid for twelve months at once.

Prices are in US dollars.

2. Free trial

Both Live plans begin with a 30-day free trial. The first charge is made when the trial ends, unless the subscription is cancelled before then.

3. What is never charged

  • No commission, and no fee per contract, per order or per trade.
  • No share of trading profits, and nothing contingent on trading activity or volume.
  • No charge on any plan to close, cancel or price a position, or to view history, analysis or PDF exports.

4. Brokerage fees

DeltaMint is not a broker-dealer. Any commissions, fees, interest or spreads charged by your broker or other third parties are separate from DeltaMint’s fees, are set by them, and are your responsibility.

5. Payment and cancellation

Subscriptions are paid by card through our payment processor. Your card details go directly to the processor; we never receive or store your card number.

A subscription may be cancelled at any time, effective at the end of the current billing period. Paper use continues free after a Live subscription ends.

6. Contact

Questions about fees or billing may be sent to support@deltamint.app.

DeltaMint is a software tool, not a broker-dealer, and does not provide investment advice. Options trading involves substantial risk of loss and is not suitable for every investor. Trades are placed through your own brokerage account, under that broker's terms.
