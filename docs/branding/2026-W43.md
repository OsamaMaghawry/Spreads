## This cycle's asks (top five, ranked by exposure)

**1. The sign-up email is the one surface with no brand at all.**
Surface: `supabase/auth/confirm-signup.html`, seen by every new user at the
moment of signup. Quote: system font stack, `color:#0f172a`, `#475569`,
`#94a3b8` (slate) and link `color:#4f46e5` (indigo). No wordmark or mark; the
only brand is the word "DeltaMint" in a sentence. Brand book accent is
`#534AB7`, text `#201B3A`, secondary `#6A6294`.
Fix: set the link to `#534AB7`, body to `#201B3A`, secondary to `#6A6294`,
and add the `deltamint` logotype line at the top.

**2. The PDF that leaves the product is titled with a forbidden name.**
Surfaces: Analysis page, exported PDF. Quote: `AccountAnalysis.jsx:416`
`title={... — Performance Analysis ...}` while the on-screen H1 (line 388) is
`— Analysis`; brand.md lists "performance page" as not-canonical for Analysis.
Fix: change the line 416 title to `${name} — Analysis` / `Analysis`.
Hand-off (not judged here): the PDF footer `DeltaMint — economic performance
report.` (`ExportPdfButton.jsx:185`, also `AnalysisDisclosure.jsx:25`) is a
third name for the same thing. Whether it is deliberate disclosure wording is
compliance-gate's call before anyone renames it.

**3. Loss and warning colours: rose/amber everywhere, the brand tokens barely used.**
Surfaces: whole app, PDF, landing. Counts in `src/**/*.jsx`: `rose-` 249,
`amber-` 202 against `dm-negative` 8 and `dm-warning` 0 (defined, unused).
Landing defines its own `--rose #B4485C` / `--amber #9A6414`; the PDF paper
banner hardcodes `setTextColor(180, 72, 92)` (= landing rose, `ExportPdfButton.jsx:130`)
and `160, 60, 60` (`:139`, `:160`), a third red. Shadcn `--destructive`
(`index.css:23`) is a fourth.
Fix: map `rose-600/700` to `dm-negative` and `amber-*` to `dm-warning` in the
Tailwind config (or a find/replace), and point the PDF reds at `#993C1D`.

**4. Primary action has no single colour on the signup-to-first-trade path.**
Surfaces: Login/Register, Connect Alpaca, close/open dialogs. Quote: Login
links and button use shadcn `--primary: 0 0% 9%` (near-black,
`index.css:15`); `AccountForm.jsx:120` (Connect Alpaca submit),
`AlpacaConnectConsent.jsx:67` (Allow), `ConfirmAction.jsx:64` use
`bg-emerald-600`; `Layout.jsx` and Admin use `dm-accent` (`#534AB7`). Brand
book: accent is "primary actions, links, focus"; positive green is "gains,
and nothing else". A green Allow/Connect button spends the gain colour on
a button.
Fix: set `--primary` in `index.css` to the `dm-accent` HSL and swap the
emerald submit buttons to `bg-dm-accent`.

**5. Landing and app disagree on the brand's own hexes.**
Surfaces: landing site, blog SVGs vs app. Quote: landing `--mint: #2E8B5F`
(light) / `#5FC48D` (dark) (`site.css:18,62`) and blog SVGs `#2E8B5F`
(`protective-put-payoff.svg`, `tail-hedge-running-ledger.svg`) against brand
book mint `#3FA672`; landing `--paper #F6F7FB`, `--line #E2E5EE` against
`#F6F5FB`, `#E1DEF2`. `--brand #534AB7` matches. The nav wordmark is
otherwise right; the colour of its "mint" half differs by site.
Fix: either set landing `--mint` to `#3FA672` or record `#2E8B5F` in
brand.md as the on-light variant (proposed rule below, vp-product's call).

## Proposed brand.md rules (one-liners for vp-product)

- Transactional email carries the `deltamint` logotype and the `dm` accent, text and secondary hexes; no system-default indigo or slate.
- Nav labels are Title Case for named features ("Option Chain", "Strategy Scanner") and register "Option Chain", "Use with Claude" as canonical names.
- A site may use a contrast-adjusted mint for text on white, but only the value recorded in brand.md; blog SVGs use the same value.
- A primary button is `dm-accent`; green and red are reserved for gain and loss and never colour a button.
- The PDF and on-screen title for a feature use the canonical name only.
