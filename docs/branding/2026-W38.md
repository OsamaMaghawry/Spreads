# Branding consistency audit — 2026-W38

Cadence: biweekly, even ISO week → light "what changed since last audit"
check against the previous audit, `docs/branding/2026-W36.md` (commit
`5ce9cd9`, 2026-09-07). Not a re-sweep of every surface — this pass verified
every W36 item's exact cited line and read the ~100 naming/voice/visual-
relevant commits (of 215 total) since then closely; items with zero related
commits are carried forward as unchanged without re-walking the surface by
hand.

Truth of claims is desk-editor's question; disclosure sufficiency is
compliance-gate's. Neither is judged here — flagged and handed off where it
came up.

`EGRESS_BLOCKED` — consistent with the existing row in
`docs/context/reachable.md` (blocked since 31 Aug). Not re-flagging; source
only this cycle again.

## What changed since W36

1. **The owner personally renamed two canonical features**, both recorded in
   `docs/context/brand.md`: "Positions Monitor" → **Dashboard** (12 Sep),
   "Screener" → **Strategy Scanner** (14 Sep). This is the reverse of what
   W36 item 2 recommended (we said unify on "Positions Monitor"; the owner
   picked "Dashboard"), but it shipped consistently: `Layout.jsx:66-67` nav,
   `Dashboard.jsx:58` H1, `Scanner.jsx:128` H1, and `landing/public/index.html`
   all agree. **W36 item 2 is now fixed**, by the owner's own decision. The
   pricing page that was the third leg of that split is no longer published
   at all (pulled 12 Sep to `landing/drafts/`), removing that surface rather
   than fixing it — see item 12 below.
2. **`brand.md` picked up a large new Voice section** (owner correction, 9
   Sep): the "trader to trader" register is out, replaced by an
   Investopedia-shaped rule — Key Takeaways, plain-question headings, one
   worked example, **900–1,200 words, hard ceiling 1,300** — written
   explicitly because the 2,143-word `credit-spread-max-loss` post was too
   long. Foundations posts 5-8 were drafted and reviewed *after* this rule
   existed and blow past it anyway (New finding A).
3. **None of W36's four proposed rules were accepted into `brand.md`.** The
   `(proposed)` red/green-semantic question at brand.md's colour table is
   byte-identical to the W36 version. No emoji rule, no lifecycle-chip rule,
   no colour-token-derivation rule exist anywhere in the current file. All of
   this cycle's brand.md changes are the owner's own naming/voice decisions —
   unrelated to what was proposed for acceptance.

## New inconsistencies found this cycle

**A. Foundations blog posts 5-8 violate the word-count rule `brand.md` was
just rewritten to enforce — and get worse with each post.**
Surfaces: `content/blog/options-bid-ask-spread.md`,
`option-delta-explained.md`, `theta-decay-explained.md`,
`implied-volatility-options-explained.md` — all drafted and reviewed
(desk-editor + seo-editor passes: `bf62ac0`, `10c3431`, `394e74f`, `32186c2`)
after the 9 Sep word-count rule existed. Total word counts: bid-ask spread
1,409, delta 1,527, theta decay 2,000, implied volatility 2,011 — against
brand.md's *"900–1,200 words, hard ceiling 1,300... the 2,143-word
`credit-spread-max-loss` is what this rule exists to prevent."* The two most
recent posts are within ~150 words of the exact article the rule was written
to stop repeating. Exposure: this is the top-of-funnel foundations series,
the primary organic-search entry point.
**Fix:** split each into two posts per the rule's own instruction ("If it
will not fit, it is two posts"), starting with theta-decay and
implied-volatility.

**B. A 6th ad hoc red in the exported PDF.** `ExportPdfButton.jsx:139`,
`rgb(160,60,60)` for the "withheld trade" banner — new this cycle, folded
into item 4 above.

**C. Email colour treatments have diverged into three, not two.** Folded
into item 10 above.

**D. Minor, not ranked.** `weeklyDigestEmail.ts` CTA button reads lowercase
`"Open your dashboard"` against the capitalised canonical `"Dashboard"` —
plausibly a generic noun, not a feature-name citation; low confidence this
is a real violation. Landing hero (`landing/public/index.html:391`) shows
the eyebrow kicker `"Scanner"` (short form) above the `"Strategy Scanner"`
H1 it introduces — brand.md's rule is the short form is fine only after the
full name has appeared, and the visual order here is reversed by one line.
Both cosmetic; not this cycle's asks.

## This cycle's asks (top five, ranked by exposure)

**1. Two parallel design systems — the trading app still ignores the `dm`
palette; unchanged for a full cycle.**
Surfaces: every trader-facing screen (`Scanner.jsx`, `Dashboard.jsx`,
`Accounts.jsx`, `AccountDetail.jsx`, `AccountHistory.jsx`,
`AccountAnalysis.jsx`, close/open dialogs). `dm-accent` still confined to
Admin + auth chrome; `Scanner.jsx:128`'s own icon is `text-emerald-600`,
`ExportPdfButton.jsx:286`'s button is `bg-emerald-50`/`text-emerald-700`.
**Fix:** repoint primary-action styling (buttons, focus rings, active nav)
at `dm-accent`; retire ad hoc emerald/near-black buttons. Same fix as W36,
now two cycles overdue.

**2. Red means "loss/risk" — now renders as six colours, not five.**
Surfaces: app-wide P/L, landing hero, the PDF (three reds in one file),
generic form errors. `dm-negative` (`#993C1D`) used once
(`OAuthCallback.jsx:109`); `rose-600`/`rose-700` at 51 sites; PDF paper
banner `#B4485C` (`ExportPdfButton.jsx:130`); new withheld-banner red
`rgb(160,60,60)` (`ExportPdfButton.jsx:139`); shadcn `--destructive`
`#ef4444` (`src/index.css`).
**Fix:** collapse every loss/risk/warning use to the `dm` scale, including
the new PDF withheld-banner colour.

**3. Foundations blog posts 7 and 8 run well over the word-count rule
`brand.md` was just rewritten to enforce.**
Surfaces: `content/blog/theta-decay-explained.md` (2,000 words),
`content/blog/implied-volatility-options-explained.md` (2,011 words) —
against the binding "900–1,200 words, hard ceiling 1,300" rule, both

**Fix:** split each into two posts per the rule's own instruction; apply the
same check to posts 5 and 6 (`options-bid-ask-spread.md` 1,409 words,

**4. Green is also decorative — unchanged for a full cycle.**
Surfaces: app-wide. The same emerald means "gain" in P/L cells and is also

**Fix:** reserve mint/emerald for P/L and connection-state truth only; move
decorative "primary action" and "in-progress" green to `dm-accent` or a

**5. The Analysis feature's PDF still carries a non-canonical name, and it's
now on every page instead of one.**
Surfaces: the Analysis page's export. `AccountAnalysis.jsx:492` still
passes `"Performance Analysis"` as the PDF title; `ExportPdfButton.jsx:184-185`
still prints `"DeltaMint — economic performance report..."`, now via
`drawFooter()` on every page (`ab47f13`), up from one page at W36.
**Fix:** change `AccountAnalysis.jsx:492` to `` `${name} — Analysis` `` and
update the footer identity string to match.
**Hand-off:** whether "economic performance report" is a deliberate

## Proposed rules for `brand.md` (silent today; re-proposed unchanged from
W36 — vp-product has not acted on these across two cycles)

- *"No emoji anywhere in product surfaces, including transactional email;
  severity is conveyed by the `dm` semantic colours and words
  (critical/warning/info), not by colour-coded emoji."* (closes item 6)
- *"Lifecycle/status chips (closed, assigned, exercised, etc.) use a single
  neutral chip style with text, not a rainbow of Tailwind hues — reserve
  colour for money and risk only."* (closes item 7)
- *"Any surface that defines its own colour tokens (landing, email, PDF)
  derives them from the `dm` hexes, not from Tailwind defaults or invented
  values."* `weeklyDigestEmail.ts` is now the in-repo proof this is easy to
  do right — the other two email templates should be made to match it.
  (closes item 8, and generalises the item 2/9 fix)
- Resolve brand.md's own open question (colour table, "(proposed)" line):
  red/green are semantic only; the app's Tailwind emerald/rose/amber/sky/
  violet usage does **not** get sanctioned as-is — it collapses into the
  `dm` scale (this cycle's items 2/4), except lifecycle-status hues, which
  get their own neutral treatment per the rule above rather than either
  meaning.
