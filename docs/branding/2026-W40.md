## Proposed brand.md rules (still silent; vp-product to accept)

- No emoji anywhere in product surfaces, including transactional email; severity is words plus `dm` semantic colours.
- Lifecycle/status chips (closed, assigned, exercised) use one neutral chip style; colour is reserved for money and risk.
- Any surface with its own colour tokens (landing, email, PDF) derives them from the `dm` hexes, not Tailwind defaults or invented values.
- Red and green are semantic only (P/L, risk, connection truth), never decorative or "primary action".
