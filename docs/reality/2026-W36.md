## 1. Money: a fully-priced pricing page exists that the product cannot back up

**Claim (docs/product/pricing.md, rewritten today 2026-09-01 14:25 UTC by the
Tuesday product cadence):** *"DeltaMint has **no published pricing**. The
product is pre-revenue... Nothing here is live until the owner says so."*

**Reality (repo, verified by reading the file):** `landing/public/pricing/index.html`
has been live-linked from the site nav since **2026-08-28** (commit `bd71272`,
four days before this org structure even existed) — three named, priced tiers:

| Tier | Price | Claimed gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paper | $0/mo | 1 paper account, 5 open positions, no iron condors |
| Pro | $39/mo | 3 accounts, unlimited positions, iron condors, continuous scan loop, 60s auto-refresh, trade history/analysis |
| Desk | $99/mo | unlimited accounts, master risk aggregation, per-strategy routing, PDF export, priority support |

**Checked against the actual code** (`grep` across `src/` and
`supabase/functions/`):
- No billing/subscription/checkout code exists anywhere (`stripe`, `billing`,
  `checkout`, `entitlement`, `paywall` — zero real matches; the only "plan"/"tier"
  hit is an unrelated test file).
- No plan/tier gate exists for **any** listed feature. Iron condors are fully
  implemented and available to everyone (`StrategyPicker.jsx`,
  `spreadPairing.ts`, `openPosition/index.ts`, etc. — not conditional on
  anything). There is no position-count cap or account-count cap anywhere in
  the code.
- PDF export (`ExportPdfButton.jsx`) exists and works for whoever can reach it
  — it isn't gated to "Desk."

**So what's actually true:** every feature on the pricing page is available,
today, to anyone with an account, for free — because there is no mechanism to
charge anyone or gate anything. The page is not "unpublished pricing that
hasn't shipped yet" — it is **published pricing, live in the site nav, for a
tier system that was never built.** Someone who signs up expecting a $39 "Pro"
paywall to eventually appear, or a competitor doing due diligence off the
public page, would be reading fiction.

**Who fixes it:** vp-product owns pricing and clearly doesn't know this page
exists — today's own pricing.md update didn't mention it. Either take the page
down (fastest, matches the "pre-revenue" story pricing.md tells) or build the
tier system it promises. This is a decision for the owner, not an agent.

**Verified by:** reading `docs/product/pricing.md`, `landing/public/pricing/index.html`,
its git log, and grepping `src/` + `supabase/functions/` for gating code.
**Not verified:** whether this exact HTML is what's actually deployed at
`deltamint.app/pricing` right now — the host is unreachable today (see §0).
Given Workers are hand-deployed and this file hasn't changed since Aug 28,
there's no reason to think it isn't live, but that's an inference, not a check.
