## Applied
I changed two search surfaces (one search title and two meta descriptions) and added two internal links, across four posts. content:check is clean on all four. The compliance gate found no hard issues. It raised three soft wording notes; I applied one (the diagonal link now says "in place of 100 shares"), and the other two were accepted as accurate.

1. **collar-option-strategy** (20 impressions, 0 clicks; "collar option" at 83.6, "covered call collar" 61, "long collar option" 57; surface unchanged since 12 Nov)
   - search_title: "Collar Option Strategy: Covered Call Plus Protective Put" → "Collar Option Strategy: Floor, Cap and the Zero Cost Collar" (59)
   - meta_description: "A collar is a covered call and a protective put on the same 100 shares. Worked example: floor, cap, break-even, and what an early assignment leaves behind." → "Collar option strategy: 100 shares, a put bought below and a call sold above. Worked example: the floor, the cap, the break-even, why zero cost is one fill."
   - Evidence: the top results for "collar option" (read 25 Nov) are Britannica ("Can you get free price protection?"), Wikipedia, Fidelity, Schwab ("What Are Options Collars?") and CFI. The zero-cost collar is the angle the results pages and their summaries lead with, and our post answers it: "zero cost" describes one fill, not the structure. The old title repeated the definition, which every result already gives. The new meta starts with the query.
2. **assignment-risk-credit-spread** (5 impressions, 0 clicks; surface unchanged since publication on 11 Nov)
   - meta_description: "Credit spread assignment risk: only the short leg is assigned. The long leg is not used for you, and exercising it or selling it settle for different amounts." → "If a credit spread's short leg is assigned, you hold a stock position and the long option, which a broker does not usually exercise for you. Two exits differ."
   - Evidence: page one for "what happens if credit spread short leg gets assigned" (read 25 Nov) is Schwab "Money Due", E*TRADE, the tastytrade help centre, TradeStation, IBKR and optionstradingiq. Searchers phrase it as "what happens if", and the results stress that the long leg is not exercised for you. The old meta's opening line, "only the short leg is assigned", reads as a guarantee. The new one answers the question as it is asked. The search title is unchanged.
3. **credit-spread-max-loss** (indexed) → **iron-butterfly-explained** (new on 25 Nov; its only inbound link was from a page with 0 impressions).
