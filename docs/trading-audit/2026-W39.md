## Findings

### 1. A covered call written against a long call (no shares) renders with a break-even of −$0.80 and "Max loss (stock to 0) $0.00" — EXECUTED (pairing), READ-DERIVED (rendering) — blocking-class default

**Files:** `supabase/functions/_shared/spreadPairing.ts` `coverExtra` (lines
617–630: `shareBasis: lot ? … : 0`), `positionKinds.ts` `breakEvenOfKind`
(line 272), `src/components/dashboard/PositionCard.jsx` (break-even leads on
stock-risk rows), `StrikeLadder.jsx` `singleGeometry` (plots any numeric
break-even).

This week the Scanner started offering covered calls against a free long call
(`3964cb6`, `95a7b3f`). In the Scanner and the ticket, that position is
treated as a spread: break-even is withheld (`buildCallOverLong`:
`breakEvenLow: null` — "Printing a number here would be inventing one"). Once
the order fills, the dashboard reads the same position through
`allocateCallCover`. The long covers the short, the row becomes a
`covered_call`, and `coverExtra` defaults the missing share basis to **0**.

**Executed (`node --experimental-strip-types`):** long IBIT 2026-12-18 40C @
$6.00, short IBIT 2026-10-16 45C @ $0.80, no shares:

```
covered_call  short 45  maxRisk 0  breakEven -0.8  collateral 0  coverLongs 1  shareBasis 0
long_option   long 40   maxRisk 600  breakEven 46
```

The card shows **Break-Even −$0.80** (it leads, because `isStockRisk`),
**"Max loss (stock to 0)" $0.00** on a position that holds no stock, and
**Capital tied up $0.00**. The strike ladder draws a "Break-even" rung at
−$0.80 and paints profit and loss zones from it. The ticket withheld the
break-even and the position card invents one, so the two screens disagree
about the same trade.

**$ consequence:** row-level only. The account totals are not understated.
The covered-call row contributes $0 and the long row $600, against the
Scanner's own bound of (6.00 − 0.80 + 0) × 100 = $520. The sum overstates by
$80, which is the safe direction. What is wrong is a nonsense break-even and a
"stock to 0" label on a position with no stock, which are the numbers a wheel
card leads with.

**Hand-off:** duty-engineer. This is a plain fix: `coverExtra` must not
default `shareBasis` to 0. Where cover is by longs only, withhold
`breakEven` (null), and do not use the "(stock to 0)" label for that row.
Giving the shape its own kind ("call over long") changes what the row means,
so that part is escalated to the founder.

### 2. The break-even that `positionKinds` deliberately withholds on an adjusted short call is re-defaulted by `syncAccounts`, and the ladder draws it — EXECUTED (the null, the adjusted-CSP figure), READ-DERIVED (the fallback, the ladder) — blocking-class default

**Files:** `supabase/functions/syncAccounts/index.ts` line 304:

```ts
breakEven: s.breakEven ?? (isCall ? leg.strike + leg.entryPrice : leg.strike - leg.entryPrice),
```

and `src/components/dashboard/StrikeLadder.jsx` `singleGeometry` (lines
53–100), which has no `adjusted` guard.

`breakEvenOfKind(SHORT_CALL_UNJUDGED)` returns `null` on purpose ("Break-even
is per share of the deliverable, and the deliverable is what a corporate
action changed"). The `??` in `singleRow` turns that null back into strike +
premium. It is exactly the "I don't know" → number substitution the house rule
forbids. The adjusted cash-secured put and the adjusted long option never
withhold in the first place: `breakEvenOfKind` computes strike ± premium
without looking at `adjusted` (executed: `AAPL1261016P00060000` @ 1.50 →
`breakEven 58.5`). `PositionCard` and `SpreadTable` print "—" on an adjusted
row, correctly. But `PositionCard` renders `<StrikeLadder spread={s} />`
unconditionally, and the ladder plots the fabricated break-even as a rung and
colours the profit and loss zones either side of it. The card's own footer
withholds the number while the ladder above it draws and colours it.

**$ consequence, worked:** short AAPL1 80C @ $1.20 after a 3-for-2 split
(delivers 150 shares). Ladder break-even is $81.20. The true per-share
break-even on the real deliverable is 80 + 1.20 × 100 / 150 = $80.80. On a
cash-and-stock or merger deliverable there is no strike-vs-spot break-even at
all. It is a coloured verdict about arithmetic we have no right to run.

**Hand-off:** duty-engineer. This is a plain fix: drop the `??` fallback
(null stays null). `breakEvenOfKind` must return null for any kind when
`adjusted`. `StrikeLadder` must draw no break-even and no zones on an
`adjusted` single row. Add a test pinning `breakEven === null` on an adjusted
single through the `syncAccounts` row builder. The builder is a closure today,
and it is untested for exactly that reason.

### 3. Adjusted contracts still reach the account totals as complete figures — now traced through condors and single legs — EXECUTED (flat, single-leg), READ-DERIVED (condor)

**Carried from W36 Finding 2, which was never ticketed.** `docs/ops/queue.md`

- **Flat spread** (re-executed): `totalRisk([{type:"put_spread", adjusted:true,
  maxRisk:183}])` → `{risk:183, complete:true}`. Unchanged since W36.
- **Iron condor** (read-derived): `syncAccounts` lines 485–486 compute
  `putSideRisk`/`callSideRisk` unconditionally. Lines 551–554 add them into
  `condorByTicker`, and the result enters `totals.risk` with no `adjusted`
  check and no effect on `riskComplete`. Same gap.
- **Single leg** (executed): an adjusted CSP `AAPL1261016P00060000` @ $1.50
  yields `collateral 6000`, `maxRisk 5850`. Risk / Equity is safe, because the

  **complete** figures. The CSP card's own "Capital tied up" cell is also

**$ consequence:** for the 3-for-2 case, the broker holds 150 × $60 = $9,000

### 4. The covered-call scan prices committed cover as if it were free: a bounded max loss, a green return and a break-even for an order that would be uncovered — EXECUTED — shipped this week (`8bff517`)

**Files:** `supabase/functions/_shared/callCover.ts` `scanCover` (a ticker
with no free cover is "priced on what it holds": all shares, all long calls),
`optionScan.ts` `buildSingle`/`scanCandidates`,
`src/components/scanner/ResultsTable.jsx`, `src/components/open/SetupPreview.jsx`.

The owner asked for in-use tickers to be shown, and showing them is right.
What is wrong is the figures they carry. **Executed:** the owner's own book,
per the comments in `8bff517` (100 TSLA, already short the 2026-10-16 390C):

```
scan: tickers [TSLA], sharesByTicker {TSLA: 100}, inUse {TSLA: "Your 100 shares already cover the 390 call …"}
candidate: 410C  maxRisk 24500  collateral 25000  sharesHeld 100  maxContracts 1
           returnOnRisk 2.04%  breakEvenLow 245  ifCalled 16500
ticket: short_call_uncovered / serious — "1 contract would have nothing behind it — … no ceiling on its loss"
```

The Scanner row shows a "cover in use" chip, and beside it **Max Risk
$24,500** and **2.0%** RoR in green. The row is ranked by that RoR among
genuinely covered candidates. The preview repeats "Max loss (stock to 0)
$24,500", "Break-even $245" and "If called away $16,500", under the sentence
"Close that call first, or this one is uncovered." As sent, the order is an
uncovered call with unbounded loss. The send-time warning is correct and
serious, and Alpaca (levels 1–3 only) should refuse the order. So the harm is
on the screen, not in the order: a bounded, confident figure is substituted
for "unbounded unless you close the 390C first."

**$ consequence:** displayed max loss $24,500 against an unbounded true loss
if the order were accepted. The RoR ranking is corrupted by a figure the
position does not have.

**Hand-off:** this changes what a number means on a surface the owner
specifically asked for, so it goes to the **founder** via duty-engineer's
escalation. My recommendation is to keep the row, withhold max risk, RoR,
break-even and "if called" (or relabel all of them "after closing the 390C"),
and sort in-use rows after free ones.

### 5. The wheel prefix mis-files single legs three ways — EXECUTED

**File:** `supabase/functions/openPosition/index.ts` line 212:

```ts
const singlePrefix = (account.wheel_client_prefix || account.spreads_client_prefix || "APP_OPEN").trim();
```

and `tradeReconstruction.ts` `classifyOrphanShort` (line 616: a `wheel`
prefix decides "covered_call" regardless of shares) and `longOnly` (line
698).

The comment's invariant ("a single-leg order from this app is always a wheel
half") stopped being true when the option chain let users buy single legs and
acknowledge an uncovered short call. Executed through `reconstruct`:

- (a) **Short call, wheel prefix, zero shares** (opened after acknowledging
  `short_call_uncovered`) → closed record `covered_call`, `unpaired:false`.
  The live dashboard calls the same position a **Naked call**, and history
  calls it a **Covered call**. Without the prefix it is correctly
  `spreads · unpaired`.
- (b) **CSP on an account with a spreads prefix but no wheel prefix** → the
  order goes out under the *spreads* prefix → filed `spreads · unpaired`, a
  cash-secured put booked as a broken spread. With no prefix at all it would
  be filed correctly as `cash_secured_put`, so the fallback is actively worse
  than nothing.
- (c) **Long call bought from the app** → wheel prefix → stored with strategy
  `wheel`, the value migration `0011` retired ("nothing writes it").

Also noted (read-derived): `tradeSync.fetchBrokerData` tests the spreads
prefix first, so a wheel prefix that begins with the spreads prefix files
every wheel order as spreads.

**$ consequence:** no dollar figure changes. These are filings, which decide
which tab and which strategy total the money lands in.

**Hand-off:** the order-side fix is mine to state and duty-engineer's to make.
Only a **short** single leg takes the wheel prefix, and there is no
spreads-prefix fallback for singles. The history side (a), where a prefix
overrides share cover on a call, concerns closed results, so it goes to
**investment-analyst**. Re-filing records already written is theirs to judge.

### 6. The wheel's adjusted share basis cannot be reached: it reads a strategy value nothing writes — EXECUTED

**Files:** `syncAccounts/index.ts` line 183 and `_shared/heldShares.ts`
line 39, both `.eq("strategy", "wheel")`. `wheelBasis.ts` line 45.

Reconstruction files wheel halves as `cash_secured_put`/`covered_call`
(migration `0011`). The only rows it still writes as `wheel` are long legs
with negative `net_credit`, which `basisByTicker` filters out. **Executed:**
a KO put sold @ $1.10 and assigned at $90, then a call sold @ $0.60 and
expired, all under the wheel prefix. Rows matching `strategy='wheel'`: **0**.
Basis as queried: `{basis: 90, source: "broker"}`. With the query reading the
two halves: `{basis: 88.30, collected: 170, source: "adjusted"}`.

This is not a wrong number on screen. Every lot is honestly labelled "broker
basis". But the feature `alert-rules.md` describes ("`_shared/wheelBasis.ts`
subtracts them") does not run for anyone. The covered-call scan's max loss
and break-even are computed on the broker basis, and the 2026-09-02
needs-owner item ("staging account has no `wheel_client_prefix`, so its
adjusted basis reads broker basis") would not fix it if the owner acted on it.

**$ consequence:** $170 per 100 KO shares of collected premium never
subtracted, stated as "broker basis". Honest, but understated.

**Hand-off:** duty-engineer. The query change is plain. Switching live basis
figures from broker to adjusted changes what a number means on every wheel
card, so that switch goes to the founder.

### 7. `alert-rules.md` against the watch as it runs: two rule gaps, one false reason, a broken table — EXECUTED (7a–c, via the real `evaluate()` sliced from `positionWatch/index.ts`), READ (7d)

- **7a. `position_oversized` never looks at shares.** `positionWatch` feeds
  `evaluate()` option legs only (`legs = positions.filter(parseOCCSymbol)`).
  Executed: 400 KO shares at 60% of $50k equity plus a small CSP → no alert.
  The doc says "one position's market value exceeds X% of account equity".
  Assigned wheel stock is usually the largest position a wheel account holds,
  and it slips past the rule entirely.
- **7b. `short_through_strike` raises a CRITICAL on a debit vertical at its
  maximum profit.** Executed: long SPY 100C / short SPY 110C, spot 115 →
  `short_through_strike/critical`. The dashboard learned `moneynessLeg` for
  debit spreads on 8 Sep and the watch did not, so the owner gets a false
  critical email on his best case. (A covered call through its strike also
  raises critical. That is assignment risk and I keep it as named.)
- **7c. An adjusted short put is priced by its root and never judged, with a
  false reason.** Spots are looked up by `occ.ticker` ("AAPL1"), so the watch
  raises `price_untrusted` "Can't judge AAPL1 — No price data." every run,
  even with AAPL trusted. Withholding is the right direction. The reason text
  is wrong, and the doc does not say adjusted puts are never judged.
- **7d. The rules table in `alert-rules.md` is broken.**
  `earnings_before_expiry` and `position_oversized` sit at lines 76–77, after
  the `price_untrusted` prose, so they render as stray pipe text. The table a
  reader sees lists five rules and the watch runs seven.

**Hand-off:** 7a and 7b are rule changes. They are code, and they go through
me and staging first; duty-engineer implements them. 7c: the reason text
should say "adjusted contract", with a matching doc line. 7d is a doc fix.
All four are tickets.

### 8. A position with no broker `current_price` is marked at its entry price and labelled "priced from the last trade" — EXECUTED (the substitution), READ-DERIVED (reach)

**File:** `spreadPairing.ts` `buildLegs` lines 50 and 77 (`p.current_price ||
p.avg_entry_price`). Executed: a KO 60P sold @ 0.90 and 100 KO @ 58, both
without `current_price` → marked at 0.90 and 58, so unrealized P/L is exactly
**$0.00**. With no NBBO mid, `PositionCard` then says "estimated — priced from
the last trade". That is false: the mark is what was paid. I could not
establish whether Alpaca ever omits `current_price` on a position. If it
never does, this never fires. The house rule does not grade defaults by how
often they fire.

**Hand-off:** duty-engineer. This is a plain fix: a missing `current_price`
is null, and the row's P/L is withheld when there is neither a mid nor a
broker mark.
