## Findings

### 1. SnapTrade option rows: the price unit is a guess, and the flag that says so is never read — EXECUTED (arithmetic) + READ-DERIVED (flag)

`snaptradeAccount.ts:56-88`. An option row's price and cost basis are assumed
to be per share (×100). The row is tagged `priceUnitUnverified: true`, and a
grep finds no reader of that field anywhere in `supabase/` or `src/`. The tag
changes nothing on screen. `optionUnitFit` exists to decide the unit but is
log-only (not wired to the rows).

Failing case: a short 100 put with `avg_entry_price` in per-contract units
(250 for a $2.50 premium) goes through `pairSpreads`:

```
avg 2.5   → cash_secured_put maxRisk 9750,   collateral 10000
avg 250   → cash_secured_put maxRisk -15000, collateral 10000
```

If SnapTrade quotes per contract, the rendered "max risk" is **negative
$15,000**. Option market value and P/L are off by a factor of 100 in the other
direction. A figure that cannot be trusted is shown as trusted, which is the
house rule violated. A negative `maxRisk` should never be rendered; it should
be withheld.

Also: `toAlpacaInfo` defaults a missing USD cash balance to `0` and a missing
equity to cash (`snaptradeAccount.ts:98-100`). Cash is not used for the
put-secured judgement (`classifyLeg` does `void cash`), but it feeds buying
power and the equity figure. That is a default where "unknown" belongs.

Fix direction: until `optionUnitFit` has confirmed the unit on a real
options-holding account, withhold option price-derived figures
(`maxRisk`, `marketValue`, `unrealizedPL`) on SnapTrade rows. Clamp or withhold
any negative `riskOfKind` result.

### 2. "Collar" label is applied without checking quantity, strike or protection — EXECUTED (staging)

`spreadPairing.ts` `tagStructures`: the first long put at or below a covered
call's strike on the same expiry tags shares, put and call as "Collar".
Nothing checks the put's contract count, the strike's distance, or whether the
shares are fully covered.

| Book | Output on staging | Truth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 100 sh, 5 long 90P, 1 short 110C | all three "Collar" | a 1:5 long-put ratio, not a collar |
| 100 sh, 1 long **10P** at $0.05, 1 short 110C | all three "Collar" | the put is worth $5; no downside protection. Shares row still shows $9,300 at risk |
| 100 sh, 1 long 90P, **3** short 110C | covered call + put + shares "Collar", with 2 naked calls beside it | a collar badge on a ticker carrying unlimited-risk calls. The naked rows stay "Naked call", so the numbers are right; the badge overclaims |

No risk figure is wrong. The label tells a trader a protection exists that the
numbers do not show. A strike-proximity and quantity check, with no label
when they fail, would be the withholding answer. The same pattern holds for
`protected_repair` (put strike compared only with `<= longStrike`).

### 3. `excessShortCalls` does not warn on a diagonal whose long expires before the short — EXECUTED (staging; same gap on `main`)

`orderWarnings.ts`: `excessShortCalls` nets calls sold against calls bought
and ignores expiry. Buy 100C Nov-20, sell 110C Dec-18 returns `null`
(no warning). After Nov-20 the short call is naked for four weeks. The
position watch already flags that exact shape
(`nakedShortCalls`, "long EARLIER expiry" → uncovered 1), so the first
warning the user gets is a critical alert after the fill, not a warning on
the ticket. The cost is unlimited upside risk on the call, and an alert
instead of a prompt. Severity: medium. The watch is the backstop.

### 4. Minor

- `excessShortCalls(..., qty: 0)` returns `contracts: 1` (`Math.max(1, …)`).
  `openPosition` passes `Number(qty) || 1`, so 0 reaches it as 1 already.
  Harmless today.
- A labelled "Covered call" can be covered only by a long call at a lower
  strike (long 100C / short 110C shown as `covered_call`, `cover: 0`, plus a
  `long_option` of risk 500 on a $300 debit). That is the known debit-vertical
  reconstruction item already in the queue. It overstates the risk, so it
  fails safe. Not re-ticketed.
- Protective put: the put's $100 and the shares' full $9,500 both appear as
  risk. The true floor-protected loss is lower. Overstates, fails safe.

## Confirmed fine (executed)

  count as cover (`sharesByTicker` drops negatives).
- 2 short calls vs 150 shares → 1 covered, 1 naked, on both the dashboard and
  the watch.
- Adjusted short call → `short_call_unjudged`, risk null, not "naked" or "covered".
- Long call at a later expiry covers the short; earlier expiry does not.
- `workingCallClaims`: sell-to-close orders are not claims; a mleg's put leg is
  not a call claim; filled units are not double counted. A resting order that
  never fills keeps cover claimed, which errs toward withholding.
- Pairing, price-trust and the max-loss/`refuseMassDelete` invariants: their
  suites pass and no new write path in this week's staging diff touches them.
