# Trading audit — 2026-W48 (Thursday, 2026-11-26)

head-of-trading ran the audit against `origin/staging` (fc29b482) and `origin/main` (0feb303d). Since 19 Nov, main has changed only blog posts, SEO and metrics, so its trading code is the same as W47's (`git log --since=2026-11-19 origin/main -- supabase src` is empty).

On staging, this week's market-facing changes were:
- the terminal on the phone (ad5ab21e, 39e75d7d, d8346356): its own ticket, a confirm step for everything that sends, stop and target handles on the chart, and extended hours by default;
- Playbook ranges and labels (663412d4);
- futures practice, replay and the challenge. These are simulated and send no orders.

The terminal, the phone included, is on staging only. But `canTrade` (`terminal/index.ts:2119`) lets the owner trade **live** accounts from it, so a staging terminal finding is live money for him.

**How each finding was checked.** EXECUTED means it was run under node `--experimental-strip-types` with the repo's own functions. READ-DERIVED means it comes from reading the code.

**Checks.** Before the fixes: `npm test` 833 JS + 893 TS green. After 8e135022: 839 JS + 893 TS green, and lint, build and `context:check` green.

## Verdict: STOP

1. **S1 (carried) is still live on main.** Main still runs the summer market clock. EXECUTED on main's `sessionPhase` for Fri 27 Nov: 14:15Z (09:15 ET) gives `open`, and 20:30Z (15:30 ET) gives `post`. Only the owner can release d365b9b to main.
2. **Holidays and half-days (F2, escalated) hit today and tomorrow.** Tomorrow the market closes at 13:00 ET. From 13:00 to 16:00 ET the terminal and the phone treat the market as open, and the terminal's stop guard is off.
3. **The phone terminal must not be released until F2 and the F1 backstop are decided.** F1 itself is fixed on staging.
