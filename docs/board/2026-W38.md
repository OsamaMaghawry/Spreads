## What shipped this week

Plain English, from `docs/ops/shipped.md`:

- **The email that told the owner he'd made "$2.7k+" for a week the app now
  shows differently is fixed at the root.** A spread's long leg, nested
  under its parent order, was invisible to the nightly history rebuild —
  it blanked 52 days of one account's stored history to "unknown" every
  night. Fixed, and stored history can no longer be silently rewritten by a
  future rebuild the same way again.
- The Account Analysis chart and the weekly digest email now measure a
  week's change from the same starting balance and explain the gap between
  "what trading did" and "what the broker's own equity did" instead of
  quietly disagreeing with each other.
- Closing a position no longer crashes to a blank white screen; the app
  gets its first error boundary anywhere.
- Reopening a saved order ticket no longer shows a false "no ceiling" risk
  warning or blank strikes (staging).
- The Scanner (formerly Screener) rename shipped everywhere a user reads
  it; a Condor now opens 1:1 by default instead of a riskier, unexplained
  2:1.
- Both app domains now serve HSTS/security headers on every response.
- The order card was rebuilt: save-for-later on any order, full decimal
  precision on quantities, clearer debit/credit labelling.
- **Not user-facing:** two broker candidates evaluated end to end —
  SnapTrade (15 of 39 reachable brokers can place an order, but the four
  biggest US options brokers are read-only and holdings routes 410), and
  Tradier (client and order translation built, nothing sent yet).
