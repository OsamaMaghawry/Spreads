## What shipped this week

Plain English, from `docs/ops/shipped.md`:

- **Billing exists and is hidden until you flip it on.** Stripe checkout, a
  billing screen, and a plan on every live account — but no Billing tab, no
  checkout button and no upgrade prompt shows anywhere until you flip
  `billing_visible`. Off by default.
- **The wheel is real.** The scanner finds cash-secured puts and covered
  calls, prices them off your actual cost basis, and can place single-leg
  orders — not just spreads.
- **Positions are typed correctly.** Cash-secured puts, covered calls,
  assigned shares and long options each show as what they are; a naked
  call is flagged with undefined risk; wheel positions carry a cost basis
  adjusted for every premium collected.
- **You can now set your own price** on opening and closing a trade —
  bid/mid/ask chips, a stepper, drag-to-price — and a resting close order no
  longer traps the ticket if you want to change it.
- **A false "naked call" alarm stopped firing** on call spreads and iron
  condors — the watch now only flags what a long or shares don't actually
  cover.
- **The pricing page stopped lying.** It no longer claims Paper lacks
  features Paper actually has.
- **The blog is a real site now:** six category hubs, breadcrumbs, RSS —
  and the landing site has a working Cloudflare deploy pipeline with
  Google Analytics and Hotjar live in production for the first time.
- **The nav says "positions,"** not the word `brand.md` forbids.

Full ledger: `docs/ops/shipped.md`, `docs/ops/2026-09-02.md`,
`docs/ops/2026-09-03.md`.
